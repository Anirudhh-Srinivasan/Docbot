# Orbit pricing and billing

Orbit has three plans, billed per member per month: Starter is free for up to 3 members, Team is **$12 per member**, and Business is **$24 per member**. Annual billing gives a 15% discount on paid plans. Prices are in US dollars and taxes may be added based on billing address.

The Team plan includes unlimited projects, GitHub issue sync, and 10 GB of file storage per workspace. Business adds SAML single sign-on, audit logs retained for 1 year, and priority email support. A 14-day Pro trial begins when a workspace is created; adding a payment method is optional until the trial ends.

Workspace owners can change plans under **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Deleting a workspace does not automatically refund charges already paid.

Invoices and receipts are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Orbit accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. To request a billing correction, email **billing@orbit.example** within 30 days of the invoice date.
