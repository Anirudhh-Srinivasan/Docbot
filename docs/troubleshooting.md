# Orbit troubleshooting

**I did not receive an invitation email.** Ask the workspace admin to check the address for typos and resend the invitation. Also check spam. Invitations expire after 7 days.

**GitHub issues stopped syncing.** Open **Settings → Integrations → GitHub** and choose Reconnect. The repository connection needs the Issues permission. After reconnecting, Orbit retries failed updates within 10 minutes; existing issues are not duplicated.

**I cannot sign in with SSO.** SSO is available on the Business plan. Confirm that your email domain matches the domain verified by your workspace owner, then select **Continue with SSO** on the sign-in page. Keep a workspace admin account available in case the identity provider is unavailable.

**The app looks out of date.** Refresh the page. If that does not help, sign out and back in; unsent draft comments are stored in your browser and may be lost when clearing site data.

For support, email **help@orbit.example** and include your workspace name, browser, and approximate time of the issue. Do not send passwords or API tokens.
